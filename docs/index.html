How it works

You're a tiny light bulb stuck in a dark old building, and the only way out is up.

Flash

Light up the room. Every crate around you casts a long, dark shadow.

Shadows turn solid

For a few seconds those shadows become real platforms you can stand on.

Climb before they fade

Walk, jump and climb your way up, then hurry before the dark melts away.

Screenshots

Six hand-made rooms, from a quiet first room to a whole factory.

Watch out for

The rooms keep finding new ways to mess with you.

Glass

Light passes straight through, so it casts no shadow.

Window light

Sunbeams wipe out any shadow they touch.

Water

Bad news for a light bulb. Mind the puddles and dripping pipes.

What's inside

A short, handcrafted puzzle platformer made for phones.

  • 6 hand-made levels
  • Beat your best flash count on every level
  • Hidden coloured bulbs to find
  • Simple touch controls
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking
  • Plays fully offline
